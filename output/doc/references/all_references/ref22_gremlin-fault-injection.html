Fault Injection

Welcome to Gremlin Fault Injection (FI)! Gremlin FI lets you use Gremlin’s comprehensive fault injection library to inject controlled failure into services, hosts, or containers. Recreate complex failure conditions, coordinate cross-functional experiments with teams, document discovered issues directly into Jira, and more.

Looking for services and reliability tests?
If you're looking for guidance on creating services and running reliability tests, see Gremlin Reliability Management (RM).
